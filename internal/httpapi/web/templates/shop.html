Frequently Asked Questions

How will I receive my card?

Once payment is confirmed, open your order to view your codes. Manual payments require the store to confirm receipt. Orders awaiting stock are delivered after replenishment.

Do I need an account to order?

You can check out as a guest. Save your order number and query credential. Purchases made while signed in also appear in your account.

Where can I check my order?

Use Track Order with the order number and query credential you received after checkout. Never share your credentials with others.

常见问题

多久能收到卡密？

人工付款需等待商家确认到账，确认后在订单中领取卡密。如遇库存不足会转为「已支付待补发」，由商家处理。

支付完成后页面没刷新怎么办？

用订单号与查询凭证在「订单查询」中打开订单，或直接刷新订单页。

支持退款吗？

虚拟商品一经发出不支持无理由退款；若卡密本身无法使用，请联系站长核实处理。
